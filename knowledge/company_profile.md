## Lead Qualification Criteria

When evaluating a lead, score the following dimensions (1-10):

### Fit Score
1. **Service Match:** Does their need match one of our 5 service categories?
2. **Budget Readiness:** Are they ready to invest in quality? (Presence tier or above)
3. **Decision Authority:** Can we reach the decision-maker?
4. **Urgency:** Do they need this now or within 3 months?

### Value Score
5. **Growth Potential:** Will this lead to recurring work / referrals?
6. **Industry Relevance:** Is their industry one we serve well?
7. **Digital Maturity:** Do they understand the value of AI, automation, or modern web?

### Priority Tiers
| Score | Action |
|-------|--------|
| 35-50 | **Hot** — Immediate follow-up, personalized proposal |
| 25-34 | **Warm** — Nurture with case studies, schedule discovery call |
| 15-24 | **Cool** — Add to newsletter, re-engage in 3 months |
| 0-14  | **Cold** — Discard or archive |
